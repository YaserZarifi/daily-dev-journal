### Overthinking at Bedtime
I've found myself struggling to fall asleep directly, my mind racing with thoughts that refuse to quiet down. It's as if my brain has a mind of its own, *constantly wandering and making it impossible to drift off*. To combat this, I've developed a strategy: I need a distraction to calm my racing thoughts.

My go-to distractions are shows like **Top Gear**, **The Grand Tour**, and documentaries such as **Cosmos**. There's something about watching engaging stories, fascinating facts, or the adventures of others that helps *ease my mind into a more relaxed state*. 

Here's how it works: as I start to feel sleepy, I begin playing an episode of one of these shows and lay down, hoping that the narrative will *slowly guide me into sleep*. It's not about being completely engaged or focused; it's about having enough mental stimulation to *keep my overthinking at bay* until I'm truly ready to sleep.

> "The mind is everything; what you think you become." 

In my case, *it's about finding the right mental stimulus* to help me transition from an overactive mind to a state of relaxation and eventually, sleep. This method may seem unconventional, but for now, *it's a strategy that works*.
