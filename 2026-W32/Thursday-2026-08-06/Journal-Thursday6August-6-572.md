### Exploring Audio Frequency Encoding for File Transfer

I recently came across a fascinating video showcasing a system built with Claude code that leverages QR codes for data transfer. The concept involved converting data into a series of QR codes, displaying them rapidly, and then scanning them with a special app to decode and transfer the data. This innovative approach sparked my interest in exploring a similar idea, but with a twist – using audio frequency encoding for file transfer.

The idea of transferring files using sound waves is intriguing, as it could potentially enable data transfer between devices without relying on Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. **This could be a game-changer for situations where traditional connectivity methods are not feasible or are restricted.**

As I delve into the research stage, I'm excited to investigate the possibilities and challenges of audio frequency encoding for file transfer. Some potential benefits of this approach include:

* **No need for Bluetooth or Wi-Fi**: Data transfer could be achieved through sound waves, making it a viable option for devices without traditional connectivity capabilities.
* **Low-tech requirements**: The only hardware required would be a speaker and a microphone, making it a potentially low-cost solution.

However, there are also potential challenges to consider:

* *Audio quality and interference*: The quality of the audio signal and potential interference from the environment could impact the reliability of the data transfer process.
* *Data transfer rates*: The speed at which data can be transferred using audio frequency encoding is unknown and may be a limiting factor.
