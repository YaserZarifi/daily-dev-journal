### A Painful Lesson in Eye Care
Yesterday, we headed to "Jermatoo" to weld the metal beams for the new house roof. In the process, I accidentally caught a glimpse of the welding flash. Last night, my eyes paid the price – severe pain, sourness, and an inability to open them. It was a miserable state.

I was fortunate that the baker's boy was around to lend a helping hand. He purchased Diclifin eye drops for me, and after applying two drops to each eye, I started to feel relief. 

> It's funny how sometimes it takes a painful experience to remind us of the importance of simple precautions.

For the first time in about five years, I went to bed early, around 10:00 PM, and woke up at 4:30 AM. Now, at 5:13 AM, I felt compelled to document this experience and share it here. 

This incident has been a *sobering reminder* of the importance of protecting my eyes. **Vigilance and preventive measures** are key to avoiding such discomfort. I'm just grateful for the quick fix, and I plan to be more mindful in the future.
