### Automating Video Posting with Cloudflare Worker

It's 12:45 AM in Ghazni, and I'm in the zone, working on my latest idea. I'm excited to share that I'm building a project using Cloudflare Worker that automates posting short videos to my YouTube channel.

Here's the concept: I send a short video (like a reel or shorts) to a Telegram bot, which then schedules and posts it directly to my YouTube channel. The goal is to maintain limits and handle the process seamlessly.

**Key Components:**

* **Telegram Bot**: Receives video files and triggers the automation process
* **Cloudflare Worker**: Schedules and handles video posting to YouTube
* **YouTube Channel**: The destination for the automated video posts

I'm looking forward to tackling the challenges of this project, including:

* Handling video file uploads and processing
* Scheduling posts to maintain a consistent upload schedule
* Ensuring compliance with YouTube's API limits and guidelines
